## 新架构总览

```
ClassDefinition
  ├── baseStats, hpPerLevel[], resistances
  ├── skillPool, activeSkillSlots
  └── breakdownPool, virtuePool

UnitData (重构后)
  ├── classData (引用 ClassDefinition)
  ├── unitName, level
  ├── primaryAttributes (Primary → Derived → Modifiers)
  ├── currentHP, maxHP, shieldHP
  ├── stress (0~200), mentalState
  ├── quirks (List<Quirk>)
  ├── equippedWeapon, equippedArmor
  ├── activeSkills (List<SkillData>)
  ├── activeEffects (List<StatusEffect>)
  ├── rankPosition (1~4)
  └── isDead, isCorpse
```
